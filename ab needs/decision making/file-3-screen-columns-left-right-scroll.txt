DECISION MAKING 3 - SCREEN COLUMNS: LEFT (PINNED) AND RIGHT (SCROLLING), PER TABLE
==============================================================================
Rule (see file10-table-layout.txt): on a table screen the first N columns are LEFT-aligned and stay pinned while the page scrolls sideways;
every other column is RIGHT-aligned and scrolls horizontally; the button column (Edit / Open / Pay) is right-aligned and is NOT pinned.
Printed documents and cards do not scroll: text is LEFT, amounts and weights are RIGHT.
Format: column heading  [table column it shows]. Worked-out columns are marked "worked out".
