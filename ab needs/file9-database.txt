TABLE: users
----------------------------------------------------------------------
column        | type                     | constraints
----------------------------------------------------------------------
id            | INTEGER                  | PRIMARY KEY, AUTO INCREMENT
username      | VARCHAR(30)              | NOT NULL, UNIQUE
password_hash | VARCHAR(255)             | NOT NULL
role          | VARCHAR(15)              | NOT NULL
is_active     | BOOLEAN                  | NOT NULL, DEFAULT True
created_at    | TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE | NOT NULL, DEFAULT now()

Table constraints / indexes:
  CHECK ck_users_role: role IN ('owner', 'accountant')
  UNIQUE INDEX uq_users_single_owner ON (role) WHERE role = 'owner'
      -> at most 1 owner
  TRIGGER trg_users_max_accountants (BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OF role, function check_max_accountants)
      -> at most 2 accountants (counts active and inactive users)

BUSINESS YEAR RULE (no table - derived from the date)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A business year runs 1 October to 30 September and is labelled by its two
years, e.g. 2025-26 = 2025-10-01 .. 2026-09-30.

Every dated record stores its year in a business_year VARCHAR(7) column:
  purchase_group, purchase_farmers_entries, purchase_individuals, broker_bookings, truck_trips, bilties, labour_work, bag_supplies, (file4-transactional.txt)
  payments, receipts (file5-payment-ledger.txt)
  (truck_load_lines, payment_allocations and receipt_allocations have no date of their own;
   they belong to the year of their parent truck_trips / payments / receipts row.)

Rules enforced by the application (app/services/years.py):
  1. Entering or changing a record, payment or receipt is only allowed when
     its date falls inside the CURRENT business year. A date in any past or
     future year is rejected, and a record already stored in a past year can
     no longer be edited.
  2. A year closes automatically on 1 October; nobody locks or unlocks it.
     The old business_years lock table was dropped (migration b7d3f0a91c42).
  3. Only the owner can view past business years (lists, detail pages, year
     report, /Years). Accountants only see the current year.
  4. Each year's data stays in the same tables, told apart by business_year.
  5. business_year is never typed by the user. The application sets it from the
     record's own date (purchase_group_date, purchase_entry_date, booking_date,
     trip_date, bilty_date, work_date, supply_date, payment_date, receipt_date):
     start year = date.year if date.month >= 10 else date.year - 1,
     label = start year + "-" + last two digits of (start year + 1).
     e.g. 2026-09-30 -> 2025-26, 2026-10-01 -> 2026-27.
     Its format is also enforced by a CHECK on every table that has the column.

TABLE: alembic_version
----------------------------------------------------------------------
column      | type                     | constraints
----------------------------------------------------------------------
version_num | VARCHAR(32)              | PRIMARY KEY (constraint alembic_version_pkc)

Not a business table. Alembic (the migration tool) keeps exactly one row here:
the id of the latest migration applied to this database. Do not edit by hand.
