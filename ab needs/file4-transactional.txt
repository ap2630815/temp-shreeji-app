Calculated by the application on save (grain and rate come from the stockyard_purchases row):
  payable_amount = ceil( (no_of_filled_bags * grain.net_bag_weight + extra_kilos) * grain_rate_per_20kg / 20 )
  due_date       = stockyard_purchase_entry_date + pay_after_no_of_days
  net payable    = payable_amount - borrowed_amount
When the rate, grain or pay_after_no_of_days of the purchase changes, payable_amount and due_date of its entries are recalculated.

NO EXACT DUPLICATES between the farmer purchase tables
Two entries are exact copies when farmer, date, grain, rate per 20 kg, filled bags and extra kilos are all equal.
Such a copy is refused when saving a purchase_individuals row, a purchase_group_farmers_entries row or a stockyard_purchase_entries row,
whether the other copy is in the same table or in either of the other two (app/services/duplicates.py). The same farmer may still
appear in all three tables in the same year with different entries.
